13. COSTS, EXPENSES, AND PROFIT

This section is very important.

Record all relevant costs, including:

- Product purchase or manufacturing cost.
- Promoter payment.
- Advertising expenses.
- Packaging costs.
- Shipping and delivery expenses.
- Discounts.
- Platform or transaction fees.
- Refunds and returns.
- Other business expenses.

Calculate the following automatically using documented formulas:

Total Revenue = Sum of valid sales amounts.

Net Sales = Sales amount after recorded discounts, cancellations, and applicable refunds, using a consistent accounting rule.

Product Cost = Cost of units sold, based on recorded unit cost.

Total Expenses = Product costs + promoter fees + advertising + packaging + shipping + platform fees + other recorded expenses, without double-counting.

Net Profit = Net Sales − Total Expenses not already deducted from net sales.

Profit Margin (%) = Net Profit ÷ Net Sales × 100, when net sales are greater than zero.

Campaign ROI (%) = (Attributed Campaign Profit − Campaign Investment) ÷ Campaign Investment × 100, when campaign investment is greater than zero.

Document exactly how campaign-attributed profit and investment are defined.

Show:

- Total revenue.
- Total expenses.
- Product cost.
- Promoter cost.
- Other costs.
- Net profit or loss.
- Profit margin.
- ROI.
- Profit by product.
- Profit by promoter.
- Profit by campaign.
- Profit by location.

Use correct calculations and avoid counting the same cost twice.

Handle missing costs, zero revenue, refunds, and negative profit safely. Never display a guaranteed profit or fabricate financial results.
